03 / Method

Straight from page 7.

OGPA = .30 LOP + .25 LOTP + .20 GWK + .15 AA + .10 ICA

LOP comes from the syllabus table. Every other category uses (obtained marks ÷ total marks) × 4. The weighted result is rounded to the nearest tenth.
